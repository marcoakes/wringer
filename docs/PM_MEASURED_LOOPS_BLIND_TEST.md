# PM blind test — measured loops

Protocol prepared 9 September 2026. **Not a test result or spending approval.**

The implementation and engineering evidence are linked from the
[measured-loop guide](native/MEASURED_LOOPS.md). Use the frozen source-build route
in [ASSISTANT_START.md](../ASSISTANT_START.md), not commands recalled from an
earlier run. Preserve the original alpha.9 baseline and all failed measurements.

## Operator preparation, before the blind phase

Record the exact Wringer commit and compiled distribution, target source commit,
v3 plan and approval digests, runtime image digest, actual adapter/model identity
when available, platform, start time and explicit finite session/time allowance.
Missing facts remain unknown. Freeze the installed bytes and entry pages.

Use a fresh private controller and disposable target branch. Existing stored
credentials may be reused: do not delete keys, reset accounts, install another
vendor or call a machine “clean” merely because its controller is new. Record
this as **pre-provisioned** unless cleanliness was independently measured.

Choose one lane before starting: owned design reference, or authorised live
Figma import. An owned PNG does not prove Figma connectivity. Confirm that the
actual browser-capable contained image, required tools and source are available;
a placeholder profile or scripted role is not a live test environment.

The primary participant must be an independent PM/designer, not the implementer
or a scripted agent claiming to be a human. Give them only the frozen
[starting page](../ASSISTANT_START.md), the connected coding app, their private job
page, their real request and approved design. Never put a private operator link
or credential value in the public report.

## The ordinary PM journey

Ask for a useful change to the Reports starter with one functional requirement
and one visual requirement. Do not supply implementation answers. Observe:

1. Can the PM describe the request, see assumptions, understand the proposed
   result, selected approach and finite allowance, then approve once?
2. Does Wringer record executed failing assertions before the worker starts?
   Does a repair receive useful failure evidence without another routine PM
   approval? If no repair is needed, report that branch as unmeasured.
3. Does the page bring the PM to the actual result with one clear next decision?
   Can they request one meaningful correction in their own words and inspect
   fresh desktop/mobile captures before deciding again?
4. Does an unavailable or stale display prevent Yes? Is a negative decision
   preserved rather than quietly reworded or bypassed?
5. After acceptance, does a separate Send publish exactly the reviewed source to
   the selected destination? Does the page supply a usable reviewer handover?
6. From a literal fresh clone, run the printed audit command. Do all carried
   claims resolve, including assertion red/green, exact approach, loop decisions
   and human notes? Then run the printed extra-breakage-test command, if offered,
   and retain its outcome without assuming it must find a defect.

Count substantive PM decisions separately from routine permission prompts,
clarifying questions, undocumented repairs and operator interventions. Record
elapsed active work, human waiting, sessions reserved/used/uncertain and both
cost lanes. Unreported billing is unknown, not zero.

An exact repeated unsuccessful version must stop before another automatic
worker. If the live run never repeats, its anti-repeat behavior is unmeasured;
the deterministic negative tests are separate evidence, not a live occurrence.

## Optional research lane — a separate decision

Do not interrupt the ordinary journey with a research experiment. After it,
an operator can connect preregistered repository-specific comparisons using the
[experiment guide](native/EXPERIMENTS.md). The PM should see the optional card on
the same page, not be sent to another dashboard.

- Review recorded results first. Confirm no model or credential-store call.
- A comparison may start only after a distinct finite research allowance. Both
  arms use fresh contained state and the same frozen source/check/runtime family;
  retain every planned, failed, timed-out, interrupted and missing trial.
- Reject or retain an inconclusive candidate. Fixture results cannot establish
  live benefit. Do not alter the prediction after seeing results.
- Only an eligible measured candidate may offer **Use for future work**. Record
  the exact version, evidence revision, actual operator and their reason.
- Check an already-approved job before and after adoption: its plan, approval
  and allowance must be identical. A future proposal may pin the new approach
  but must still seek its own execution approval.
- Restore the previous approach, attempt a stale decision, and confirm that
  rollback changes future selection only. An unknown or out-of-scope result
  must not become a recommendation or a green claim.

Real blinded designer observations require genuinely hidden arm identities and
held-out material outside proposer/worker clones. A checked box, model score or
invented display hash is not a substitute. Research observations and private
experimental handovers never grant production Yes or Send.

## Verdict and capture

The blind verdict ends at the first undocumented hand repair. Capture that stop
whole, including the page and exact words. Label any continuation **salvage** and
log every repair; do not improve the app mid-test or merge the phases afterward.

Publish pass, fail or inconclusive with denominators, not selected highlights:
entry pages touched; commands and outputs with secrets removed; all stops and
recovery; PM hesitation and actual comments; reservation/cost/time records;
selected playbook and adoption identities where present; certificate, board,
summary, reviewer note, bundle inventory, audit transcript and breakage result.
List unmeasured branches explicitly. A successful engineering rehearsal does
not pre-judge this independent verdict.
