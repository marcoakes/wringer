# Code of conduct

Wringer's issues, discussions and pull requests are for building a tool people
can trust. Behave in a way that makes that possible.

- Be respectful and specific. Criticise work, evidence and claims, not people.
- Report what actually happened. Do not invent results, transcripts or reviews,
  and say when something was run by an AI agent rather than a person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks,
  and no publishing anyone's private information.
- Keep secrets, keys and exploit details out of public threads. Security reports
  go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules,
and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately,
open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/marcoakes/wringer/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report.

This policy draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
